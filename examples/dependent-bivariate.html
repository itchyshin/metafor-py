Two correlated outcomes per study

Question: How can two outcomes share a study-level covariance while their sampling errors are correlated?

The synthetic two-outcome case supplies the sampling covariance matrix and fits an unstructured 2 × 2 study-level outcome covariance. The benefit and harm labels define the model-matrix columns.

Run the native example from the repository root:

python examples/dependent.py --case=bivariate

The generated comparison reports both outcome means, the variance components, their covariance and correlation, and coefficient covariance. See the local comparison page.

Data and provenance

The fixture contains generated data that demonstrate a Berkey-style two-outcome structure. Its provenance, generation script, and checksums are in reference/dependent/v1/PROVENANCE.md.

Differences from R metafor

The unstructured covariance is implemented for two outcomes on the checked fixture. Three or more outcomes and other structures are unsupported; see COMPATIBILITY.md.
