Supplied-correlation phylogenetic fits

Both ports and the pinned R reference fit a species random intercept with a supplied labelled correlation matrix and diagonal sampling variances. The first fixture estimates a boundary variance; the second estimates an interior species variance. The inputs and R references are synthetic and versioned beside each fixture.

Tolerance for every displayed quantity: 1e-5 + 1e-4 × |R|.
